WHY SAFEVOICE

Designed around privacy and responsible case handling

SafeVoice is designed to provide a structured digital platform for reporting domestic violence while allowing authorised administrators to review and manage submitted cases.

Confidential Reporting

Reports are accessible through authenticated accounts and authorised administration.

Evidence Support

Users can attach supporting files to their incident reports when appropriate.

Case Status Tracking

Users can monitor whether their report is pending, reviewed or resolved.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology for structured incident reporting

SafeVoice is a web-based domestic violence reporting platform developed to provide a structured method for documenting incidents and submitting them to authorised administrators.

The platform includes user authentication, email verification, incident reporting, evidence upload, case-status tracking, administrator management and PDF report generation.
